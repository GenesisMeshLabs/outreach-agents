# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Get Genesis Mesh into the strongest possible position for EIC Accelerator funding.

Core capabilities:
- cryptographic identity
- capability-based authorization
- delegation
- revocation
- auditability
- cross-organization trust
- multi-cloud and bare-metal interoperability
- operational sovereignty

Human

# POSITIONING

Do NOT position Genesis Mesh as another AI-agent framework.

Position it as infrastructure required when autonomous systems operated by different organizations must cooperate securely.

Core thesis:

"Europe does not achieve AI sovereignty simply by hosting AI in Europe. Sovereignty requires the ability to verify, limit, revoke and replace every provider without losing control of authority."
